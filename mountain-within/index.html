For the one far from home.

Sooner or later most of us leave a place that made us. These films are for the hard part of the crossing: the first weeks, the long gray months, the season nobody outside can see. Watch them in order, or start where you are.

Good marks, and a heart that is still at home, counting the days.

From the outside, everything in order. Behind the door, the hardest season of your life.
A hard day, anywhere.

The message you did not want. The exam. The meeting. The news from home.

Two minutes. Try it now.

Take a small stone, or whatever is in your hand. Keep it in your hand. Let the jaw go soft and the shoulders drop. Then breathe with the ring: the breath out longer than the breath in, so the heart slows and the thinking mind comes back.

If you are struggling right now.

You are not a burden, and you do not have to carry this alone. People are awake right now whose whole work is to listen.

  • In the United States and Canada, call or text 988, any hour.
  • In the United Kingdom and Ireland, call Samaritans free on 116 123, any hour.
  • Anywhere else, findahelpline.com shows a free line in your country.
  • If you are a student, your campus counseling center is there for exactly this. Walk in or call tomorrow morning.
  • Keep working with your doctor. Never stop or change a medication on your own.
  • If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

And tell one person who loves you, tonight. Let them light your way.
