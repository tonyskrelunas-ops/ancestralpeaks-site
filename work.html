For teams, companies and boards.

The same seven steps, run by a leadership team, a company or a board.

Six readings for a team or a board

Each reading holds a passage, the principle, questions for the room, and one practice for the quarter. Nothing sacred is in them, only what works and belongs to no one.

  1. Goals: the house, not the star. Markets, products and roles are stars, useful while low, then gone. Know which house you are steering by, and count four kinds of wealth at once: financial, relational, cultural and ecological.
  2. Structure: the relay. Nobody carries the whole way. Short legs, and all the care in the hand-off, said back before the runner lets go.
  3. Meetings: a room where people can think. Settle the room before anything is decided. Two channels in every report, the numbers and what they mean.
  4. Governance and the board: the One Question. Will this make the great-grandchildren whole, or hollow them out? Leaders recognized, not campaigned for. Decisions woven, not voted to a winning half.
  5. A mindset of abundance: fullness is equipment. A room moves away from the one who needs something from it.
  6. A mindset of discipline: calm is stored, not summoned. Built on quiet days, spent on hard ones.

The Crossing: a team exercise

Tables of five take the parts of a championship team: the coach, the carrier, the steady one, the wild one who plays every monster, and the Eagle, the ancestor watching from above. Each table writes three true sentences for a far community, then carries them past the monsters every crossing meets: panic at the dry spring, ego and pride at the arrival.

Halfway, the carrier is told to give two of the three sentences away, so no one carries it alone. At the end comes the line the exercise turns on:

That is the message. It is not mine. I only carried it.

Then the gap between what was written and what arrived, a real first step with a person and a date, and finally the tables carry their messages to one another.

Formats: a 90-minute core, a half day, or a full day with body sessions and sound. Localized for companies (the far community is a customer or the field), boards (the stakeholders and the next board) and community teams (the families served). Facilitators train as the one the coach calls in, so the exercise scales beyond any one teacher.

We are the far messengers now.
