Questions

Who are the guides? Characters from the stories, each a master of one walk of life. They guide the films and the practice. The live circles are real people.

Is this therapy? No. It is practice. If something stirs more than you can carry, bring it to the circle or to someone who can help.

Do I have to be from a particular people? No. Every people on earth once raised far messengers. Every one of us has them in our line.

We are the far messengers now.
