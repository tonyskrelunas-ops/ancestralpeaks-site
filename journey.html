Before week one

The first crossing: the Journey West.

Forty-five minutes in which you become a far messenger.

Your first crossing

Before week one, every member makes one crossing. Not a lecture about far messengers: forty-five minutes in which you become one.

You leave from a stream with a stone in your hand. A song pulls west. You carry the people's gratitude across the whole hard country to the ones at the edge of the water who still keep the ways, the ones who still sing and still plant. You walk as a watcher, reading the wind and the clouds. You meet the eagle on the high ground, sit at a fire with one of the wise ones who crossed before you, cross the hard country, find the spring dry, and remember where the other water is.

Gratitude is the only cargo that gets lighter the more of it you carry.

On the fourth day, something changes, and it is not the country.

The world did not change. The relationship did.

And at the far end, the people on the shore are looking up the trail you came down.

You were expected.

How members receive it

A 25-minute and a 45-minute recording, guided in a calm, unhurried voice, with the stone in your hand the whole way. Come back to it whenever the road gets long. It ends where every crossing ends:

abundance isn't the feast waiting for you. It's what you bring to it.

We are the far messengers now.
