In Stone Breath, Masan says it this way: “As the host, you prepare. You are always supposed to provide food.” So before anything else, here is what is set out for you. It is open now, and it stays open for three months.

In the Listening Room: three sessions in full: Settle yourself first, The Pocket and The Gratitude Meditation. Morning Water, eleven minutes by the water, is always open.

Three voice notes: about a minute each: Go running early; Under sixty beats; Pick up a pebble, run east.

For the day: three short guides, Before the First Step, Before You Pull Out and Setting the Day Down, and a ten-minute workout, Out the Door.

For the ones who carry others: Peak State, the dawn guide in Heal the Healer.
