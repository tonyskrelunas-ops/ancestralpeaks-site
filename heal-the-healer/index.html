    A person who was carried, who is now carrying, for people they will not meet.

    Tony Skrelūnas, The Far Messengers

    That is all a far messenger ever was. Not a hero. When something had to cross hard country, the old peoples did not send the fastest runner. They sent the one who could arrive with it whole. If you spend your days holding other people steady, on a ward, in a classroom, in a company, at a kitchen table, you are in that line now.

    And if you are tired, hear this from the same book: it did not make you weak. It made you unable. You do not make the calm out there. You spend what you already stored. These guides are a place to store some, made from the tools in Stone Breath: a small stone in the hand, a counted breath, a few plain questions.

      The runner who never rests cannot run home.

      Tony Skrelūnas, Stone Breath

      Rest is not the reward for the work. It is the root of it. The field that gave everything is left fallow so it can give again. Discipline that costs you sleep is not discipline. It is depletion wearing discipline's clothes.
