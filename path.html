The path

Eight weeks. The way they actually trained.

Before week one

Every member begins with the first crossing, the Journey West: forty-five minutes in which you become a far messenger.

The eight weeks

One training a week, in the order every tradition used: settle the carrier first, find what holds, store it, check it, share the load, bring it home, and tie it to something that returns. Each week has a reading, the science in five minutes, seven days of practice, a reading in text and audio, a short film, and a circle.
